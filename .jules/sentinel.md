## 2025-02-25 - [Add Dependabot]
**Vulnerability:** Outdated dependency updates
**Learning:** Repositories without dependabot configuration files are susceptible to unpatched supply chain vulnerabilities.
**Prevention:** Ensure `.github/dependabot.yml` exists to keep packages secure.
